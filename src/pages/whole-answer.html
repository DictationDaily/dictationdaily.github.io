⚙

Turn it on

The ⚙ gear at the bottom left of a lesson, then Show the whole answer.

📝

Press Enter

The whole sentence shows under the box, with every mistake. What you typed stays in the box.

✏️

Fix it

The first wrong word is selected. Type over it, fix the rest, and it checks by itself.

The colours

  • ❌ Red, crossed out: a wrong word you typed
  • ✅ Green: the right word
  • ➕ Green on light green: a missing word
  • ⚪ Grey: words the lesson gives you

Questions

What is “Matching 5/7 words”?

How many words of the sentence you got right.

Do I turn it on for every video?

No. It stays on for every video on this phone or computer, until you turn it off.

Turned off, what do I get?

The usual hint: one mistake at a time. See Red and green words.

Is it in Shadowing?

No. In Shadowing you say the line, so there is nothing to type.
