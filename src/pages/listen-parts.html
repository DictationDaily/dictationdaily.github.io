Why some parts aren't exercises

A lesson plays the whole video, but not every moment makes a fair dictation. These become Just listen parts:

  • music, applause or silence
  • very short lines, like “Hello.”
  • another language
  • people talking over each other
  • speech that's too fast or unclear to type
  • stretches with no transcript

Gaps shorter than about a second between two clips are left out, so you don't get tiny listen parts.

What happens during a listen part

  • The transcript is shown if there is one, and the line being spoken is highlighted.
  • A bar shows how much of the part is left.
  • It plays once, then the next exercise starts by itself.
