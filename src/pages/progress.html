Progress is only saved when you're signed in. It's stored in your account, so it's the same on every device you sign in on.

What gets saved, and when

Something is saved each time you answer a clip correctly, the moment you see You are correct!:

SavedUsed for
Your place in that video (the end of the clip you just answered)Resuming, the red bar on thumbnails, My Learning
One practice record: the date and the time you spent on the clipLearning time, active days, streaks

Skipping, replaying, watching, or typing without getting the clip right saves nothing.

Picking up where you left off

Open a video you've practised before and the lesson starts right after the last clip you answered correctly, including the listen part that follows it, if any.

💡
Your saved place loads a moment after the page opens. If you press START instantly, the lesson may begin at clip 1. Wait a second, or reload and try again.

Which clip is saved

Your saved place is the last clip you answered, not the furthest one you reached. For example:

  1. You answer clips 1 to 30. Saved place: after clip 30.
  2. You go back with ◀ to clip 5, press Undo and answer it again.
  3. Saved place: after clip 5. Next time, the lesson opens at clip 6.

To get back to where you were, press ▶ (or Continue) through the clips and answer the last one again. Redo the lesson works the same way: the old place is replaced as soon as you answer clip 1.

Practising on several devices

Your place is stored in your account, not the browser, so you can start on a computer and continue on a phone. If two devices practise the same video, the most recent correct answer wins.

Signed out

Without signing in, every lesson starts from clip 1 and nothing is recorded. Practice done before signing in can't be added later.
