Rest the mouse

After a moment, a small card shows above the word: its kind, how to say it, and its meaning in your language and in the language you learn.

Phrases

Some words mean something else together (“se passer”, “start off”). Their words turn orange and the phrase shows in an orange box.

🔖

Save

Move onto the card and press the bookmark to save the word. For the phrase, open Everything about this word: the Part of a phrase here box has its own bookmark. Both are practised in Words.

Questions

Does the video stop?

No. The video plays on and your keys keep working: any key, or moving away, puts the card away.

I want the whole card

Click the word, or Everything about this word on the small card. See Look up a word.

Nothing shows when I move across a sentence

That's on purpose: only a word you stop on opens.

On a phone?

Phones and tablets have no mouse: tap the word for its card.

How do I practise a saved phrase?

In Words, it is a card marked New phrase: type its words in the gaps (Tab goes to the next gap), or say the whole stretch.

Which words open a card?

Real words of a finished sentence, also in “Just listen” parts. Names, numbers and sounds like “euh” don't.
