🔁 1. Listen again

Each sentence plays 3 times. Then it stops. Press Tab (or Replay) to hear it again, as often as you want.

🐢 2. Slow it down

Play the video at half speed. The sentence still repeats.

1. Click ⚙

At the top right of the video.

2. Click Speed

It says “Normal” now.

3. Click 0.5x

Or 0.75x.

🧩 3. Go word by word

  1. Type only the words you heard.
  2. Press Enter. The hint shows the next word in green.
  3. Type it, listen again, and press Enter.

⏭️ 4. Skip it

Press Esc (or Skip). You can come back later with the ◀ arrow. See how.
