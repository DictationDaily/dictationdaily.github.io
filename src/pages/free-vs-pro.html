How daily free practice works

Dictation, Shadowing and flashcard review share one daily allowance. Switching modes does not give you a new allowance. See Settings for your current daily minutes and plan.

When your time runs out, you can finish the sentence, line or card already on screen. The next one waits until your local midnight, when your free allowance starts again. Pro removes this daily time limit.

Which plan should I choose?

Free: a little every day

Practise listening and whole-sentence speaking, save words and review flashcards within your daily allowance.

Pro: keep going

Practise for longer, look up meanings and examples in your language, and check the pronunciation of individual words.
