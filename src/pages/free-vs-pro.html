A couple of useful details

Do I need an account?

You can try a lesson without an account. Sign in with Google for the Free features shown here, including saved words, translations and progress.

What happens when my free time runs out?

The next sentence, Shadowing line or flashcard waits until your local midnight. You can keep going with Pro, which removes the daily practice time limit.
