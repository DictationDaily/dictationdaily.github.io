Hidden settings

Things most learners don't know

  • 🐢Slow the audio down to 0.5×
    The clip still loops at the slower speed.
  • 🔗Filter by level on your phone
    Type the filter into the address, e.g. ?level=b1.
  • 📍Redoing an old clip moves your saved place
    Your bookmark is the last clip answered, not the furthest.
  • ▶️Press play on the video to start
    The YouTube play button works just like START.
