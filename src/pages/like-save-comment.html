Save puts the video in Practise later, your own private list, or in one of your playlists.

Comments

Ask about a word or a line, or share a tip. Quote the line adds the line you just answered.

Questions

What does 👎 do?

A small card asks what didn't work (too fast, too hard, too easy, wrong subtitles, boring topic, bad audio): one tap, or Skip. Only we see it. The video is not suggested to you again, and your practice card takes the reason into account.

Can I take it back?

Tap the same thumb again.

Where are the videos I saved?

Playlists › Your playlists › Practise later. They also come near the top of your practice card until you practise them.

Will a comment give the answer away?

No. A quoted line from further in the video than you have got stays hidden: “Hidden until you get to 9:40”.

Who sees my comment?

Everyone: with your first name, your Google picture and your streak, never your email. Delete your own with ⋯ › Delete; ⋯ › Report tells us about someone else's.

Close the comments

The ‹ at the top, the comments button, or a click on the darkened lesson.

Do I need to sign in?

To like, save and comment, yes. Anyone can read comments.
