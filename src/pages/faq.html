General

What is Dictation Daily?

A listening practice site. You watch real YouTube videos one sentence at a time, type what you hear, and get word-by-word feedback. It trains your ear for natural speed, linking and accents.

Do I need an account?

No. Every lesson is open to everyone. Signing in with Google adds saved progress, learning time and streaks. Compare.

Which level should I choose?

Start with B1 Intermediate and the Slow sort. If you get most clips right in one or two checks, move up a level. If you need to replay every clip many times, move down or slow the audio.

How many videos are there?

Over 1,800, from TED, BBC Learning English and Great Big Story. Nearly all have a CEFR level from B1 to C2.

How long should I practise each day?

Ten to twenty minutes daily works better than one long session a week. One correct clip is enough to keep your streak.

Can I add my own YouTube video?

Not yourself. Videos need a timed transcript before they can be turned into exercises. You can open any library video by its YouTube ID, though: see how.

Practising

The clip stopped repeating.

Each clip plays up to 3 times on its own, then waits. Press Replay or Tab to hear it again, as often as you like.

Can I change how many times a clip repeats?

No, it's fixed at 3. Use Tab for extra replays.

The ▶ button at the bottom is greyed out.

You can only go forward once the current clip is answered correctly (or the listen part has played). Use Skip (Esc) to move on without answering.

Why does it say “Just listen” with nothing to type?

That part of the video isn't a fair exercise: music, a one-word line, another language, overlapping voices or very fast speech. It plays once and the next exercise starts. Press Enter to skip it. More.

How do I redo a clip I already answered?

Go back to it with ◀ and press Undo. If you're signed in, note that this moves your saved place back to that clip once you answer it.

How do I restart a lesson from the beginning?

Finish it and press Redo the lesson, or press ◀ repeatedly to get back to clip 1.

Tab doesn't replay the clip.

The text box needs to be selected. Click inside it, then press Tab. During listen parts, keys work anywhere.

Enter doesn't start the lesson.

Before the lesson starts, Enter only works once you've clicked the instructions box. Simplest: click Start, or press play on the video.

Close took me to a different website.

Close works like your browser's back button. If you opened the lesson from a link elsewhere, it goes back there. Use the DD logo to reach the library.

Answers and hints

My answer is right but it says it's wrong.

Look at the green word in the hint: it's what the transcript expects. Common causes:

  • numbers written differently (30s vs thirties, 10 vs ten)
  • British vs American spelling (colour / color)
  • filler words the speaker really says (um, so, like)
  • one word vs two (every day vs everyday, well known vs well-known)
  • your phone's autocorrect changing a word
Do capital letters and punctuation matter?

No. Capitals, commas, full stops, question marks, quotes, and apostrophes or hyphens inside words are all ignored. Full rules.

Why does it only show one mistake at a time?

So you listen again for each word instead of copying the answer. Fix the first mistake and check again; the next one is shown.

What does “Matching 5/8 words” mean?

The first 5 words in a row are right, out of 8 words in the clip. It counts from the start up to your first mistake.

The transcript itself has a mistake.

Transcripts are made automatically from the audio, so they're occasionally wrong. Type what the hint says, or skip the clip.

Can I see the answer without typing it?

There's no reveal button. Type what you can and press Enter: each check shows the next correct word, so you can build the answer word by word. Or skip the clip.

The hint disappeared.

It hides when you type or replay, so you can concentrate on listening. Press Enter to check again and see it.

Settings and hidden features

Can I slow the audio down?

Yes. Click the ⚙ gear on the video → Speed → 0.5x (or use − for 0.75x). The clip keeps looping at that speed. With screenshots.

Can I speed it up for a challenge?

Yes, the same Speed menu goes up to 2x.

Can I turn on subtitles?

No. Captions are switched off during lessons, and turned off again if you enable them, so the answer isn't shown on screen. After a correct answer you see the full transcript with each word highlighted as it's spoken.

Is there a settings page?

No. Speed and quality are in the YouTube player's ⚙ menu. Everything else is fixed. See what.

How do I filter by level on my phone?

Add it to the address: dictationdaily.com/videos?level=B1. You can add &duration=under-4-minutes and &sort=Slow too. All options.

Can I bookmark a filtered list?

Yes. The address bar always holds your current sort and filters. Bookmark it or share it.

I found a video on YouTube. Is it on Dictation Daily?

Copy the ID after v= in the YouTube address and open dictationdaily.com/videos/<ID>. If it's in the library, the lesson opens; otherwise you'll get “page not found”.

Can I start with the video's play button?

Yes. Pressing ▶ on the YouTube player starts the lesson, just like Start.

What are the highlighted words after a correct answer?

Each word lights up while it's being spoken. Press Tab to replay and follow along. It's great for hearing how words join together.

How can I use less mobile data?

Choose ⚙ → Quality in the player and pick the lowest resolution. Only the sound matters for dictation.

Is there a dark mode?

Not in the app at the moment. This guide has one: use the moon button in the top bar.

Progress and streaks

Why did my lesson start from clip 1 again?
  • You weren't signed in. Check for your avatar in the top right.
  • You pressed Start before your saved place had loaded. Reload and wait a second.
  • You hadn't answered any clip correctly in that video yet. Skipped clips aren't saved.
My lesson reopened at an earlier clip than I reached.

Your saved place is the last clip you answered, not the furthest. Re-answering an earlier clip moves it back. Explained here.

Why is my streak lower than I expected?

Streak days change at midnight UTC, not your local midnight, so a late or early session may count for a different day. A day only counts if you answered at least one clip correctly. Check your time zone.

Do I lose my streak at midnight?

No. If you practised yesterday, your streak stays until the end of today. It resets only after a whole day without a correct clip.

Why are the Current Streak dates backwards?

They're shown most recent first: “Oct 17 - Oct 6” means a streak from 6 October up to 17 October.

My learning time seems low.

Only active time counts: from when a clip appears to your last keystroke, for clips you answered correctly. Idle time and skipped clips aren't counted. Details.

My statistics went back to zero.

The My Learning statistics cover the current calendar year and restart on 1 January. Your video list and saved places are kept.

Does my progress sync between my phone and computer?

Yes, as long as you sign in with the same Google account on both.

Account and privacy

Can I sign in without Google?

Not at the moment. You can practise without signing in; your progress just isn't saved.

My Learning is empty after signing in.

Progress belongs to the email you signed in with. Open the account menu (your avatar) to check which account you're using, and switch if needed.

How long do I stay signed in?

Up to 30 days on the same browser, even after closing it.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. Answers are checked in your browser. Only “clip answered” and the time spent are saved, and only when you're signed in. Privacy details.

How do I delete my data?

Contact the site owner. Signing out doesn't delete anything.

Devices

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Tap the text box to type and use the on-screen buttons instead of shortcuts. Phones and tablets.

Why doesn't the keyboard open by itself on my phone?

On phone-sized screens the text box isn't selected automatically, so the keyboard doesn't cover the video every clip. Tap the box when you're ready.

Can I use fullscreen?

You can, but the text box and hints are hidden while the video is fullscreen. Exit fullscreen to type.

Which browsers are supported?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on computers, phones and tablets.
