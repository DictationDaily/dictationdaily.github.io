Questions

Does it change lessons too?

Yes. Every page turns dark: videos, lessons, Shadowing, Words and flashcards, Settings. The videos themselves don't change.

Do I choose again on my phone?

Yes. The choice is kept on each phone or computer, not in your account.

I'm not signed in. Can I use it?

The site follows your device: dark when your phone or computer is set to dark. Sign in to choose yourself.

What does Device mean?

Your phone or computer decides. Many phones turn dark at night by themselves, and the site turns with them.
