The translation under each sentence

When you sign in, the app also asks Which language do you speak? From then on, every sentence you finish shows its translation in your language, under the sentence, with a small flag.

  • In Dictation, the translation appears when the sentence is right. Never while you type, so it can't give the answer away.
  • In a “Just listen” part, each line shows its translation while it plays.
  • In Shadowing, it appears after your take is checked.

Not signed in? The translation's place says Sign in to view translation.

⏱
The first time can take a moment. If nobody has opened a video in your language before, its translation is made when you press Start: the first sentences are ready in a few seconds, the whole video in under a minute. You can start straight away.

Hide or show translations

In the lesson's bottom bar, next to Close, tap the flag (you can see it in the picture above, at the bottom right). Tap it again to show them. The flag is only there when the video has a translation in your language.

One word, not the whole sentence?

With Pro, tap any word of a finished sentence to see what it means here, hear it, and read its dictionary entry: Look up a word.

Change your languages

Click your picture at the top right, then Settings › Languages:

  • I speak: your language. Translations and word meanings use it.
  • I'm learning: which videos the library shows. You can also use the Language menu on the videos page.
  • Translations: on or off.

Good to know

  • Your language can be Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese or English.
  • Translations and word meanings are for signed-in learners. Without an account you can still do every lesson.
  • The translations are written by AI. They are good, but not perfect.
