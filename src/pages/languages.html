Questions

Do I need an account?

Yes: translations are for signed-in learners. Without an account the sentence says Sign in to view translation.

I don't see a translation

Check Settings › Languages: I speak is your language and Translations is on. A video opened for the first time in your language needs a few seconds.

Which languages can I read translations in?

Twelve, chosen under I speak, among them Tiếng Việt, 中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, العربية, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português and English.
