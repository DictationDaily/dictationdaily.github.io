Questions

What is “Part 3 of 9 · 10 sentences · about 5 min”?

A video is practised in parts of about 5 minutes, counted at your own speed. The card shows the next part, with a small bar of the video's parts (green = done). A short video says Whole video.

Why this video?

The line under it says why, for example Because you liked “…” or You saved it for later.

How do I get easier or harder videos?

Just practise. Get sentences right on the first check and the videos get harder. A 👎 with Too hard or Too easy moves them a little too. There is no test, and the site never tells you “your level”.

How do my 👍 and 👎 change it?

👍 brings more videos on that topic and from that channel. 👎 means that video is never suggested again; the reason you pick (too fast, boring topic…) changes what comes next. See Like, save, comment.
