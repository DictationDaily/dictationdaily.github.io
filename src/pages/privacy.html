What is stored

DataWhy
Your name, email address and profile picture link (from Google)To show who is signed in
For each video you've practised: your place and when you last practised itResuming lessons, My Learning

Nothing is stored for visitors who aren't signed in: a lesson saves nothing while you're signed out.

What is never stored

🔒
The text you type is checked in your browser and is never sent anywhere or stored. Only the fact that you got a clip right, and how long it took, is saved.
  • Your Google password. Sign-in happens on Google's own page.
  • Your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Dictation Daily only asks for name, email and picture.
  • Clips you skipped, or answers that were wrong.

YouTube

Videos are embedded from YouTube, so YouTube's own player and privacy terms apply to the video itself.

Deleting your data

To have your account data deleted, contact the site owner. Signing out does not delete your data; it just removes the sign-in from that browser.
