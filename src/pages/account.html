Signing in

  1. 1
    Click Sign in in the top right of any page.
  2. 2
    Google's sign-in page opens. Choose your account, or enter your Google email and password.
  3. 3
    Allow Dictation Daily to see your name, email address and profile picture. Google asks this each time you sign in.
  4. 4
    You're back on Dictation Daily, with your picture in the top-right corner.

You stay signed in on that browser for up to 30 days, even if you close it.

Signing out

Click your avatar in the top-right corner. The menu shows your name and email. Click Sign out.

Signing out on one browser doesn't sign you out on others.

Using more than one Google account

Progress belongs to the email address you sign in with. A different Google account gets its own, separate history. If My Learning looks empty, check which account you're using in the account menu.

Signing in without Google

There's no other sign-in method at the moment. You can still practise everything without an account; only progress isn't saved.
