See all your words

Press See all words under the chart, or New, Learning or Known above it.

Questions

What do New, Learning and Known mean?

New: saved, not practised yet. Learning: you are practising it. Known: you should still remember it three weeks from now.

Why is my chart flat at the start?

It counts the words you had on each day. Before you saved your first word, there is nothing to count. Words you practised before 16 November 2026 count from their last practice.

Can I search my words?

Yes. On the list, type in Search your words: a word, its meaning, or a word of its sentence.

Remove a word

Open the word in the list, press ⋯, then Remove word. Undo brings it back.

Which language?

The language of the flag at the top. Switch the flag to see your words in another language.
