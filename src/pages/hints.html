The kinds of mistake

What matters?

These are all OK:

✅ Capital letters ✅ Commas and full stops ✅ Apostrophes (dont = don't) ✅ Numbers (19 = nineteen) ✅ Money ($5 = five dollars) ✅ British or American spelling (colour = color) ✅ Spaces and hyphens (a lot = alot) ✅ Names and sounds like “um”: any spelling, or leave them out ✅ Notes nobody says, like “[Carmen]”: leave them out

Be careful with:

⚠️ Spelling ⚠️ Small words like “a”, “the”, “to” ⚠️ Word endings (-s, -ed)
