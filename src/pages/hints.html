Reading a hint

When you check an answer, the app compares it word by word with the transcript and stops at the first word that doesn't match.

You seeMeaning
Matching 2/8 wordsHow many words in a row you got right from the start, out of the words in the clip.
ixA word you typed that is wrong or extra.
isThe word that should be there.
...More words follow that aren't shown yet.

The three kinds of mistake

  • Wrong word: the word is selected in the box. Just type the right one.
  • Missing word: the green word is shown and the cursor is placed where it goes.
  • Extra word: a word you typed that isn't in the clip is selected so you can delete it.

The words before the mistake are also rewritten in the transcript's spelling and capitals, so you can see the correct text growing.

ℹ️
Only the first mistake is shown each time. A clip with three mistakes takes at least three checks. The hint disappears as soon as you start typing again.

What counts as the same word

Before comparing, both your word and the transcript word are lower-cased and stripped of everything except letters and digits. So:

talentstalents,✅ punctuation ignored
dontdon't✅ apostrophes ignored
wellknownwell-known✅ hyphens inside a word ignored
(nothing)--✅ symbol-only “words” ignored on both sides

thirties30s❌ numbers must be written the same way
colorcolour❌ spelling must match
💡
Not sure how a number or name is written? Type your best guess. The hint will show the transcript's version, which you can copy.

When you're stuck

  • Replay (Tab) to listen again. It also hides the hint so you can concentrate.
  • Slow the video down with YouTube's speed setting. See Slow down the audio.
  • Skip (Esc) to move on. Skipped clips don't count as done and aren't saved.

When the transcript is wrong

Transcripts are created automatically from the audio, so occasionally a word is wrong. Type what the hint says, or skip the clip.
