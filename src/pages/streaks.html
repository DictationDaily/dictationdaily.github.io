How learning time is counted

For every clip you answer correctly, the app counts the time from when the clip came up to the last key you pressed before the correct check.

Clip appears → listening → typing → fixing hints
counted
last keystroke → Enter
not counted
  • Listening, typing and fixing mistakes count.
  • Time after your last keystroke doesn't, so leaving the tab open doesn't inflate it.
  • Skipped clips don't count at all.

It's a measure of active practice, not of time with the page open.

Active days

A day is active when you answered at least one clip correctly on it. One clip is enough.

Streaks

A streak is a run of consecutive active days.

  • Current Streak: the run that includes today or yesterday.
  • Longest Streak: your best run this year.

You have until the end of today

Your streak isn't lost at midnight. If you practised yesterday, the streak still shows yesterday's count all day today, and goes up when you answer a clip. It drops to 0 only after a whole day without practice.

Days are counted in UTC

Days start and end at midnight UTC (Coordinated Universal Time), not your local midnight.

💡
In Ho Chi Minh City, practice at 06:30 still counts for the previous day. In New York, practice at 20:00 already counts for the next day. Practising once around the middle of your day is the safest way to keep a streak.
