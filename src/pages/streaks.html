The rules

✅

One sentence is enough

Get one sentence right and the day counts.

🌙

You have all day

Your streak doesn't stop at midnight. You have until the end of today.

💔

Miss a whole day…

…and the streak starts again from 0.

⏱

Only active time counts

Listening and typing count. Time away from the keyboard doesn't.

When does a new day start?

Where you are, a new streak day starts at

07:00

The app uses world time (UTC), not your local midnight. Your goal minutes use the same days.

💡
Practise in the middle of your day, and you'll never lose a streak by accident.
