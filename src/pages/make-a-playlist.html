Start from a video

On any video, press ⋯, then Save to playlist and New playlist. Add more videos the same way: tick your playlist.

👀

Who can see it

Private: only you. Anyone with the link: friends or your class. Public: anyone, in Explore (3 videos or more).

🔗

Share it

On your playlist's page, press Share to copy its link or post it on Facebook. Friends can open it without an account.

Questions

Where is the New playlist button?

On a video, as on YouTube: ⋯ › Save to playlist › New playlist. A playlist always starts with a video.

How do I add more videos?

⋯ › Save to playlist on a video, then tick your playlist. A note at the bottom right says Saved to …. Or press Add videos on your playlist's page.

How do I change the order or remove a video?

On your playlist's page, drag a video (on a computer), or press its ⋯: Move to top, Move to bottom or Remove.

How do I rename it or delete it?

On your playlist's page, the ⋯ beside Share: Edit (name, about, level, who can see it) or Delete.

Can I change someone else's playlist?

No, but you can copy it: ⋯ › Make a copy on their playlist gives you your own to change.

Is there a limit?

50 playlists, and 200 videos in each. All the videos of a playlist are in one language.
