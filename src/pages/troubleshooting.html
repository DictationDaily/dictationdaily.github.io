No sound, or the video won't play

  1. Check that the browser tab isn't muted and your volume is up. The YouTube player has its own volume button too.
  2. Some browsers block sound until you've clicked on the page. Click Start with the mouse once.
  3. Check that YouTube works in another tab. If it's blocked on your network (school, office, some countries), lessons can't play.
  4. Turn off extensions that block YouTube embeds, then reload.

“This video is unavailable”

The video's owner removed it from YouTube or stopped it being embedded on other sites. Pick another video.

“Dictation exercises for this video are not available yet”

This video doesn't have a transcript yet. You can watch it, but there's nothing to dictate. See the screen.

The clip plays the wrong part or keeps jumping

If you drag the video's progress bar outside the current clip, it jumps back to the clip. That's on purpose. Use ◀ / ▶ in the bottom bar to change clips.

“Couldn't load videos. Please try again.”

The library couldn't reach the server. Press Retry. If it keeps happening, check your connection and reload the page.

“Something went wrong”

Press Try again. If that doesn't help, reload the page. It's usually a short network problem.

“This page could not be found.” (404)

The address is wrong, or the video isn't in the library. Go to dictationdaily.com/videos.

Google shows an error when I sign in

  1. Try again in a private or incognito window.
  2. If it still fails, report it to the site owner with the exact error message.

My progress isn't being saved

  • Check you're signed in: your avatar should be in the top right.
  • Progress is only saved when you get a clip right, not when you skip.
  • Make sure you're using the same Google account as before.
