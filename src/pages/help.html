🔇 I can't hear anything
  1. Check that your volume is on.
  2. Check the volume button on the video.
  3. Click Start with your mouse. Some browsers need one click first.
  4. Check that YouTube works in another tab.
📺 “This video is unavailable”

The video was removed from YouTube. Please choose another one.

📄 “Exercises are not available yet”

This video has no sentences to type yet. You can watch it, or press Back to browse.

⏪ The video keeps jumping back

That's normal. The video stays on the current sentence. Use ◀ and ▶ at the bottom to change sentence.

⌨️ Enter or Tab doesn't work

Click inside the text box, then try again. Before the lesson starts, just click Start. If a word card is open, close it first (Esc).

🌍 I don't see a translation

Translations are for signed-in learners: is your picture at the top right? Then check Settings › Languages: I speak is your language and Translations is on. In the lesson, the ⚙ gear at the bottom left opens a menu with a Translation switch: check it is on. A video opened for the first time in your language needs a few seconds.

💾 My progress isn't saved
  1. Is your picture at the top right? If not, sign in.
  2. Wait one second before you press Start.
  3. Only correct sentences are saved, not skipped ones.
  4. Use the same Google account as before.
🔄 “Couldn't load videos” or “Something went wrong”

Press Retry or Try again. If that doesn't work, reload the page.

🔍 “We can't find that page”

The link is wrong, or the video was removed. Press Browse videos.

🔑 Google shows an error when I sign in

Try again in a private window. If it still fails, tell the site owner the error message.

Report a bug

Something looks wrong in a lesson? Tell us from the app. You need to be signed in.

In a lesson, click the ⚙ gear at the bottom left. It is under the Translation switch. Not in a lesson? Use Report a bug at the bottom of any page, or in the menu under your picture. One sentence is enough, for example which word was marked wrong. A picture of the screen, the last minute of the app's log, the page and your device. Untick Include the screenshot to leave the picture out. We read every report.
