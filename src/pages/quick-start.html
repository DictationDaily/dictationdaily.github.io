Go to dictationdaily.com and press Start now. Click any video you like. The first sentence plays. The sentence plays up to 3 times. Want to hear it again? Press Replay. Don't worry about capital letters or commas. If a word is wrong, it turns red. The right word is green. The wrong word is already selected. Just type the right word and press Enter again. Press Continue (or Enter) for the next sentence.
