Step by step

Go to dictationdaily.com/videos and click any video. The first sentence plays. The first and last words are already there. The red line fills while the missing words play. Only the words in the gap. Capital letters and commas don't matter. A wrong word is red. The right word is green. Type over it and press Enter again. When all the words are right, the ✓ comes by itself. Press Enter for the next sentence.
