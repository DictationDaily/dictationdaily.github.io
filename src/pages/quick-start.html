Step by step

Go to dictationdaily.com and press Start your first lesson. The first time, it asks which language you want to learn. Click any video. Short ones are best to start. The first sentence plays. The first and last words are already there. The red line fills while the missing words play. Only the words in the gap. Capital letters and commas don't matter. A wrong word is red. The right word is green. Type over it and press Enter again. When all the words are right, the ✓ comes by itself. Press Space for the next sentence. Under the sentence, in your language. Without an account it says Sign in to view translation.
