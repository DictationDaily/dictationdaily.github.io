Sorting

The three buttons above the grid change the order.

💡
Slow is the best way to start if fast speech is hard for you. It ranks videos by how many words per minute the speakers say.

Filtering by level

Click a level in the left panel to show only videos at that level. Click it again to turn it off.

Filtering by length

Duration has three options, based on the full YouTube video length:

  • Under 4 minutes: a quick session (a 2-minute talk has about 45 clips).
  • 4–20 minutes: most TED talks.
  • Over 20 minutes: long lessons and box sets.

Combining filters

You can combine one sort, one level and one duration. Click the Level or Duration heading to collapse that group.

Bookmarking and sharing a list

Your sort and filters are stored in the address bar, so you can bookmark a list or send it to a friend. The browser's back button steps through your filter changes.
