Everything works on a touch screen. The video sits above the dictation area, and the bottom bar stays at the bottom of the screen.

Typing on a phone

  • On screens narrower than 600 pixels (most phones held upright), the text box is not selected automatically, so the on-screen keyboard doesn't keep popping up over the video. Tap the box to type.
  • Use the Check, Replay and Skip buttons instead of the keyboard shortcuts.
  • Your phone's autocorrect can change words. If an answer keeps failing, check the hint for a word autocorrect changed.

Filters on a phone

The filter panel is hidden on phones; sorting still works. To filter by level or length, type it into the address: see filters in the address bar.

Tablets

Tablets get a two-column library. Screens 600 pixels wide and up behave like a computer: the text box is selected automatically when a clip starts, so the on-screen keyboard opens by itself. With a keyboard attached, all the shortcuts work.

⚠️
Avoid the video's fullscreen button during a lesson: the text box and hints are hidden while the video is fullscreen.
