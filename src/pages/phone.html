Phone tips

🎧

Use headphones

You'll hear small words much better.

✍️

Watch autocorrect

Your phone may change a word. Check the red word in the hint.

🔎

Find a video

Topics, search, Quick win, Sort and the filters work like on a computer. Swipe the topics sideways.

↔️

No fullscreen

In fullscreen you can't see the text box.

🔥

Your streak

The flame is at the top. Tap Progress to see your goal and numbers.

⌨️

The keyboard waits

Tap the box when you want to type. Use the Replay and Skip buttons.
