Questions

What do the squares mean?

One per sentence: ✓ right on the first check, a number for how many checks it took, – for a sentence you skipped.

What can I do with the words?

Rest the mouse on a word for its meaning, or tap it for its word card, and Save it to practise later in Words. In Shadowing, the card lists the Words to say again.

What happens after the last part?

Last part done!, then FINISH opens the finish screen with your streak. The video is already saved as finished.

Is anything saved from the card?

No. It is worked out from what you just did. Your place is saved after every sentence, as always.

Do the parts change?

Once you have started a video, its parts keep their numbers.
