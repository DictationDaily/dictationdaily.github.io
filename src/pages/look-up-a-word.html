Questions

When can I tap a word?

Once the sentence is right (in Shadowing, once your take is checked). Names, numbers and sounds like “euh” aren't tappable.

What is on the card?

The word, its kind and forms, how to say it (IPA), Listen (a native speaker), In the video (the speaker), the meaning in your language, Say it, and all its meanings with examples.

Is Say it an accent grade?

No: it shows how easily the word was understood, and each sound as a green, brown or red chip. Speaking softly or far from the microphone can lower it. One- and two-letter words may not be checked.

Close the card

Esc, the ✕, or a click outside. On a phone, swipe it down. The video waits while it is open.

A meaning looks wrong

Press Report a mistake at the bottom of the card. Meanings are written by AI; recordings come from native speakers on Wikimedia Commons.
