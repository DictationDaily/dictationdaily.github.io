Tap a word

When you have finished a sentence (in Dictation, once it is correct; in Shadowing, once your take is checked, or on a line you did before), every word in it can be tapped. While a Shadowing line plays or you are speaking, its words can't be tapped, so nothing stops the line or the microphone. On a computer, the dictionary opens as a panel on the right. The lesson behind it goes darker but doesn't move, so you are back exactly where you were when you close it.

What the card shows

🔤

The word

The word as a dictionary lists it, what kind of word it is (and whether a noun is masculine or feminine), how to say it in IPA, and the forms you need: the article and plural, or a verb's main forms.

🔊

Hear it

Listen says the word clearly. In the video plays the moment the speaker says it, then goes back to where you were.

🎯

Meaning

What the word means, in your language, with a simple explanation in the language you are learning.

🎙️

Say it

Say the word yourself and see how easily it was understood. More below.

📚

All meanings

The word's common meanings, each with two short examples and their translations. The meaning used in your sentence has a red number.

💡
Not every word. Numbers, names of people and places, and sounds like “euh”, “ah” or “haha” aren't tappable: a dictionary has nothing to say about them.
💡
Small words and expressions. In French and Italian, “l'homme” or “dell'anno” are two taps: the article and the word. A word that is part of an expression, like “pick … up” or “fängt … an”, is looked up as the whole expression.

Say it

Under the meaning there is a green box with a microphone. Tap it and say just the word, once. Recording stops by itself when you go quiet. A moment later you see the word and a score from 1 to 100:

🟢

85 and up: Great

“Easy to understand.”

🟢

70 to 84: Good

“Understood.”

🟠

Under 70: Try again

“Hard to understand. Listen, then try again.”

Yours plays your recording, so you can compare it with Listen (the word said clearly) and In the video (the speaker). Say it again records a new try. If you didn't say anything, the box says We didn't hear anything and nothing is counted.

💡
What the score means. A speech-to-text model listens to your recording, and the score is how sure it was of what it heard. A high score means you were easy to understand. It is not an accent grade: a clear word with an accent can still score well, and saying it softly, too fast or far from the microphone lowers the score. To work on how it sounds, play Yours right after Listen and copy what you hear.

The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. If you said no, the box says Allow the microphone: allow it in your browser's site settings and try again. Say it shares its daily number of checks with Shadowing. Your recording is checked and then dropped: nothing is stored, and nobody listens to it.

While the card is open

The video pauses and the lesson's keys wait, so Space doesn't move you on by mistake. Esc, the ✕, or a click outside closes the card, and you carry on where you were.

On your phone

On a phone the card slides up from the bottom of the screen. Swipe it down or tap above it to close it. In Say it, Yours and Say it again are the round ear and microphone buttons.

Without Pro

On the free plan, tapping a word opens a short card instead: the word with a PRO badge, Upgrade to Pro to look up words and a Join the Pro waitlist button. Once you have joined, it says On the waitlist.

Good to know

  • Looking up words is part of Pro. The meanings are in the language you chose under Settings › Languages › I speak.
  • While you listen, the words of the sentence you are on are prepared, so their cards open at once. You can meet up to 500 words a day that nobody has looked up before; words already in the dictionary never count.
  • The cards are written by AI and can contain mistakes. If something looks wrong, press Report a mistake at the bottom of the card.
