The bottom bar

“Exercise 2/42” means sentence 2 of 42. Go to the sentence before. You see your answer. The box is empty again. Listen and type! Works after you get the sentence right. Leave the lesson (on a phone it is a ✕). Or click the logo at the top to go to the videos.

“Just listen” parts

Some parts have nothing to type, for example music or very short words. The next sentence starts by itself. Press Space to skip, or tap SKIP on a phone.

The end of the lesson

After the last sentence the card celebrates: Lesson done! or, signed in, N-day streak! with your numbers for the day. The video plays on.

  • Find another video: back to the videos.
  • See my progress → (signed in): your streak, goal and time.
  • Redo the lesson: start again from sentence 1.

Without an account you see your sentences and minutes. More about the streak: Your streak.

Open a video you already finished

Signed in, a video you finished on another day opens on a short card instead: You've done this one, with the day you finished it. Choose what to do next:

  • Practise again (or Enter): type all the sentences again from the start.
  • Say it in Shadowing: say the same sentences out loud (Shadowing, Pro).
  • Find another video: more videos at the same level.

The celebration only shows when you finish a video in this visit.
