Skipping a clip

Press Skip or Esc to go to the next clip without answering.

A skipped clip isn't saved and doesn't count towards your learning time. You can always come back to it with ◀.

Going back to an earlier clip

  • Clips you've already answered show their transcript, and don't replay automatically.
  • Press Undo to clear the answer and try it again.
  • Press ▶ or Continue to move forward again.
⚠️
If you're signed in and re-answer an earlier clip, your saved place moves back to that clip. See Which clip is saved.

Leaving a lesson

Close takes you back to the page you came from, just like the browser's back button. If you opened the lesson from a link in another app or site, Close takes you back there. Click the DD logo instead to go to the library.

When you're signed in, nothing is lost: your place was saved at your last correct answer.

Redoing a whole lesson

At the end of a lesson, press Redo the lesson to start again from clip 1. While signed in, your saved place switches to the new run as soon as you answer the first clip.
