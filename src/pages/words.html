Questions

Where is Words?

In the menu on the left (on a phone, under ☰). It lists every word you saved, with its sentence and how well you know it: New, Learning or Known.

What is on a flashcard?

The sentence from the video with your word missing, its translation in your language, and the word's meaning. After you answer, Listen and Slow read the sentence, and a link opens the lesson at that sentence.

I wrote it without the accent

It counts as right: the letter shows brown, so you see which accent to fix. For letters like é or ç, press the Éç key, or hold Alt (Option on a Mac) and press the key shown.

I don't remember the word

Press Show. Then type it once to go on. The card comes back a few cards later.

How many cards a day?

Words that are due come first, then up to 10 new words a day. A practice is up to 20 cards. Practice time counts for your streak.

Remove a word

On Words, press ⋯ beside it, then Remove word. Undo brings it back. Pressing Save on the word card again takes it out too.

Daily free practice

Every signed-in learner can save words. Reviewing flashcards shares your daily free practice time with Dictation and Shadowing. Finish the card on screen when your minutes run out; the next card waits until your local midnight. Pro gives you unlimited practice time.
