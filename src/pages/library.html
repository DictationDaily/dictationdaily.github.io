The library is at dictationdaily.com/videos. Open it with Start now on the home page, or by clicking the logo on any page.

Reading a video card

  • The thumbnail, with the video's length in the corner.
  • A red bar under the thumbnail once you've started the video (signed in only). Its length shows how far you've got.
  • The title and channel.
  • The YouTube view count and how long ago it was published.

Click anywhere on a card to open the lesson.

Loading more videos

The library shows 24 videos at a time. Scroll to the bottom and press Load more for the next 24. The button disappears when there are no more videos for your filters.

⚠️
If the list can't load you'll see “Couldn't load videos. Please try again.” Press Retry. It's almost always a short network problem.

On a tablet or phone

The grid shrinks to two columns on a tablet and one on a phone. On phones the filter panel is hidden; see filters in the address bar for a way round that.
