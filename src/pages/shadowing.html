Dictation trains your ears. Shadowing trains your mouth.

Listen to one line, say it right after the speaker, and see which words were heard clearly.

Switch to Shadowing

Open any lesson. In the top bar, click Shadowing. The video stays; only the card on the right changes. Each mode remembers its own place, so you can switch back to Dictation at any time.

One line in four taps

▶️

1. Listen

The line plays and the words light up. Press it again as often as you like, or press Tab.

🔴

2. Record

Tap the red button (or Space) and say the line. It stops by itself, or tap again.

✅

3. Compare

Words that were heard clearly are green; the rest are red. Speaker and Me play the two takes.

➡️

4. Again or Next

Again records a new take. Next line (or Enter) moves on.

💡
Lines, not clips. Shadowing uses whole sentences, or natural phrases of a long one. When a sentence is cut into phrases, Play the whole sentence lets you hear the full rhythm first.

What the score means

The score is the share of the line's words the app heard clearly in your take. 100% means every word was clear. It is not a pronunciation grade: speaking too fast, too softly, or too far from the microphone also lowers it. Under the score you also see your timing, for example You said it 0.8 s slower than the speaker, and a hint such as Try “cup of” again.

Too fast? Slow it down

Press 0.75× under the card to hear the line slower. Lines from fast speakers start at 0.75× by themselves. The speed only applies to Shadowing.

It counts for your streak

Each line you finish counts as practice, exactly like a correct dictation sentence: it adds to today's minutes and keeps your streak alive. Shadowing keeps its own place in each video, separate from your dictation progress.

On your phone

Shadowing works on phones too: the player is on top, the card under it. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.

Your privacy

Your recording is sent to a speech-to-text model to find out which words were said, and then dropped. Nothing you record is stored, and nobody listens to it. Each account can run a number of checks per day; after that, recording and playback still work, and the word check comes back the next day.

Something's not working?

The record button says microphone access is needed

Allow the microphone for dictationdaily.com in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.

“This browser can't record audio”

Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

“We didn't catch any words”

Record closer to the microphone, or check which microphone your system uses. Then press Again.

“We couldn't check this take”

The speech service didn't answer. You can still play your take next to the speaker's, or record again.
