Dictation trains your ears. Shadowing trains your mouth.

Listen to one line, say it right after the speaker, and see which words were heard clearly.

Switch to Shadowing

Open any lesson. In the top bar, click Shadowing. The video stays; only the card on the right changes, and it keeps Dictation's shape: REPLAY at the top, the sentence in the same box, the action under it. Press START (or Enter); the browser asks for the microphone once, and the first line plays. Each mode remembers its own place, so you can switch back to Dictation at any time.

One line in four taps

🎧

1. Listen

The line plays by itself and the words light up. REPLAY (or Tab) plays it again; 0.75× plays it slower.

🎤

2. Say it

When the line ends the microphone opens by itself: a short sound plays and the button turns green, with a small wave inside that moves with your voice. Say the line at your pace. It stops by itself once you go quiet (or tap it, or Space), with a lower sound.

✅

3. See the result

Green words were heard clearly, red underlined ones were not. With a mother tongue set, the line's translation appears under it, as in Dictation. The ear button plays your take; the red button opens the microphone again.

➡️

4. Continue

CONTINUE (or Space) moves on, and the next line plays by itself.

💡
The same lines as Dictation. A Shadowing line is a Dictation sentence: the same cut, the same text and the same translation under it. Parts that aren't clear speech (music, other languages, unclear words) aren't lines, but they aren't skipped either: they play as Just listen stretches, like in Dictation, and move on by themselves. Space skips one.

Going back to a line

The bottom bar's ◀ takes you to an earlier line. A line you have already done opens quietly, marked Done: the line and its translation are there to look at, nothing plays and the microphone stays shut. REPLAY (or Tab) plays it, the red button (or Enter) records it again, and CONTINUE (or Space) moves on.

What the score means

The score is the share of the line's words the app heard clearly in your take. 100% means every word was clear. Names and sounds like “um” stay grey: they are not checked and don't count. It is not a pronunciation grade: speaking too fast, too softly, or too far from the microphone also lowers it.

Too fast? Slow it down

Press the 0.75× button to hear the line slower; press it again for normal speed. Lines from fast speakers start at 0.75× by themselves. The speed only applies to Shadowing.

It counts for your streak

Each line you finish counts as practice, exactly like a correct dictation sentence: it adds to today's minutes and keeps your streak alive. After the last line you get the same finish screen as Dictation, with SHADOW IT AGAIN. Shadowing keeps its own place in each video, separate from your dictation progress.

On your phone

Shadowing works on phones too: the player is on top, the card under it. The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.

Your privacy

Your recording is sent to a speech-to-text model to find out which words were said, and then dropped. Nothing you record is stored, and nobody listens to it. Each account can run a number of checks per day; after that, recording and playback still work, and the word check comes back the next day.

Something's not working?

The record button says microphone access is needed

Allow the microphone for dictationdaily.com in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.

“This browser can't record audio”

Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

The score is 0% and the ring never moved

The microphone heard nothing. Record closer to it, or check which microphone your system uses, then tap the red button for another go. The button turning green is the sign it is open.

Recording stops before I finish

It stops after about two seconds of quiet. Keep the pauses between your words shorter than that; tapping the button stops it only when you want.

The start screen says SIGN IN TO START

The checks need an account, and a free one is enough: sign in with Google and the START button comes back. If a result says “Sign in to have your takes checked”, your session ended: sign in again and record the line once more.

On iPhone the microphone doesn't close by itself

Tap the green button when you have finished. It closes on its own at the latest a few seconds after the line's length.

“We couldn't check this take”

The speech service didn't answer. You can still play your take next to the speaker's, or record again.
