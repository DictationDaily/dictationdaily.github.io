Keys

Enter starts and plays your take, Esc tries again, Space stops recording or moves on, Tab replays the line.

How is the score counted?

Each word is checked for pronunciation. A green word counts fully, a brown word half, a red word not at all. Speaking too softly or far from the microphone lowers it too.

Recording stops before I finish

It stops after about two seconds of quiet. Keep pauses short, or tap the button when you are done.

The microphone dot stays on

Expected: the microphone opens once at START so lines don't stutter. Nothing is recorded until the button turns green.

“We couldn't check this take”

The speech service didn't answer. Press UNDO to try again.
