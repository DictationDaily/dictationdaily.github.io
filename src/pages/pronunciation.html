Questions

Is it an accent grade?

No. It shows how easily a speech model understood you. Speaking softly, far from the microphone or with noise around lowers it too.

Which words are checked?

Names and sounds like “um” are grey and don't count. Words of one or two letters may not be checked, and a word heard as one that sounds the same (“mes” as “mais”) counts as understood.

How do I compare with a native speaker?

On the word card, Yours plays your take and Listen a native speaker. In a sentence, the ear next to the ring plays your take and Tab replays the video.
