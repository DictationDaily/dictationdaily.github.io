Questions

How many videos can I add?

A video uses its length in minutes: up to 30 minutes a day and 120 a week, one video up to 30 minutes long. Your picture › Usage shows what you have used and when it resets.

The video is already a lesson

It is added to your videos at once and uses none of your minutes.

What if it can't be made?

If the speech isn't clear enough for dictation (music, people talking at once, another language), we email you and give your minutes back. A video still in line can be cancelled too.

Who can see my lesson?

It is unlisted: it is on your Add a video page and in your History, and anyone with its link can open it, but it is not in the library.

Which languages?

English, French, German, Italian, Spanish or Portuguese.
