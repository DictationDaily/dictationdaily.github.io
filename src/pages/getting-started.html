What you need

💻 A browser

A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Phones and tablets work, but a keyboard is much faster for typing answers.

🎧 Sound

Headphones are best. Videos play from YouTube, so YouTube must work on your network.

⏱ Ten minutes

A short daily session is enough. A 2-minute video has about 45 clips.

🔑 Optional: Google account

Only needed to save your progress. See Signing in.
