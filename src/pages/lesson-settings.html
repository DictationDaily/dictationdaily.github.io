Cover subtitles

Some videos have the words in the picture. The glass blurs them, so you listen instead of read. On by default.

↕️

Move or resize the cover

Drag the glass, or pull its top or bottom edge. RESET puts it back. Press DONE.

Questions

What do the colours of the whole answer mean?

Red and crossed out: a wrong word you typed, with the right word in green after it. Green on light green: a missing word. Grey: words the lesson gives you. Matching 5/7 words counts the words you got right.

Do I set it for every video?

No. Both switches stay as you set them for every video on this phone or computer. A cover you moved is kept for that video only.

I don't see Cover subtitles

Only videos with the words written in the picture have it.

And in Shadowing?

Shadowing's gear has only Translation and Report a bug: there you read the line out loud, so nothing is covered.
