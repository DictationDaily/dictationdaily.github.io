➕

Make your own

On any video, press ⋯, then Save to playlist and New playlist. A playlist always starts from a video.

🔗

Share it

On your playlist's page, press Share to copy its link or post it on Facebook. Friends can open it without an account.

✅

Pick up where you are

Continue opens the video you're in the middle of. A video you finished anywhere has a ✓ in every playlist.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for everyone. Sign in to make or save a playlist. Anyone with a link can open a shared playlist and play it.

Where are my playlists?

Playlists in the menu on the left (☰ on a phone), tab Your playlists: the ones you made and the ones you saved. Until you have one, the page opens on Explore.

How do I add more videos?

The same way: ⋯ › Save to playlist on a video, then tick your playlist. A note at the bottom right says Saved to …. Or press Add videos on your playlist's page.

How do I change the order or remove a video?

On your playlist's page, drag a video (on a computer), or press its ⋯: Move to top, Move to bottom or Remove.

Who can see my playlist?

Private: only you. Anyone with the link: friends or your class. Public: anyone can find it in Explore (it needs at least 3 videos). Change it any time with Share.

How do I find other playlists?

Playlists › Explore: search by name, or pick a level (A1 to C2). Typing in the search on Home also shows matching playlists above the videos.

What is the bar above the exercise?

It shows the playlist and where you are (Video 1 of 39). ⏭ skips to the next video, ⌄ shows the whole list. When you finish a video, press Next video.
