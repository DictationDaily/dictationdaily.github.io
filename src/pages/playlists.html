Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for everyone. Sign in to save a playlist. Anyone with a link can open a shared playlist and play it.

Where are my playlists?

Playlists in the menu on the left (☰ on a phone), tab Your playlists: the ones you made and the ones you saved. Until you have one, the page opens on Explore.

How do I find other playlists?

Playlists › Explore: search by name, or pick a level. Typing in the search on Home also shows matching playlists above the videos.

How do I go to the next video?

A video you open from a playlist stays in it. When you finish it, the finish screen shows the playlist and where you are (Video 3 of 39), ⌄ for the whole list, and Next video.

What is Practise later?

Your own private list, like YouTube's Watch later: Save in a lesson, then tick Practise later. It is in Your playlists. See Like, save, comment.

Can I make my own?

Yes, from any video. See Make your own playlist.
