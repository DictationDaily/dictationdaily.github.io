Questions

Do I set it for every video?

No. Your choice is kept on this phone or computer, once for videos with words in the picture and once for the others.

Where did the glass over the subtitles go?

It is gone since November 2026: nothing may be drawn over the YouTube player. The small player does the same job.

Is it in Shadowing?

No. Shadowing shows the line's words anyway, so it opens side by side.

On a phone

The small player stays above the exercise. On a sharp screen you may still make out the words: try not to look!
