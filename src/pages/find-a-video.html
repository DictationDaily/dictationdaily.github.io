Questions

What is on a video card?

The level (B1–C2) at the top left, the length at the bottom right, and the channel and speed (Slow, Normal, Fast) under the title. Signed in, an orange bar or Done shows your progress.

Where are the videos I started?

Signed in, at the top of the videos page: Pick up where you left off. All of them are in My videos.

The list ends
