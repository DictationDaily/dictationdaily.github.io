Step by step

Click a topic, for example Science. All topics shows everything again. Type a word from the title or the channel. The list changes as you type. Click Quick win · under 5 min. You can finish a whole video today. Click Sort. Slowest speaker first is great when fast speech is hard. Each button opens a short list. Choose one option. Turns all your choices off. The lesson opens. Press Start!

Which level am I?

Click Level and choose one.

🌱

B1 · Intermediate

Clear speech about everyday topics.

🌿

B2 · Upper intermediate

Longer talks, normal speed.

🌳

C1 · Advanced

Talks about complex ideas.

🏔️

C2 · Proficient

Fast, natural speech. A real challenge.

💡
Not sure? Start with B1. If it feels easy, go up one level. Not every video has a level yet, so you can also choose Speed: Slow.

What's on a video card?

  • 🏷️ Level (B1 to C2), top left
  • ⏱ Length of the video, bottom right
  • 🗣️ Speed: Slow, Natural or Fast
  • 🟧 Orange bar or Done: your progress (when signed in)

Want more videos?

Scroll to the bottom and press Show more videos.

When you're signed in

  • At the top: Pick up where you left off. All my videos → opens My videos. See them.
  • Sort: For you puts videos for you first.
  • Status starts on Not finished, so finished videos are hidden. Choose All videos to see them.
