Questions

What is on a video card?

The level (A1–C2) at the top left, the length at the bottom right, and the channel and speed (Slow, Natural, Fast) under the title. Signed in, an orange bar or Done shows your progress.

What is in Filters?

Level, Length, Speaker speed and, signed in, Status (not started, in progress, finished). The list changes as you click. Each filter on shows as a small label, like B1 ×: click it to turn it off.

What is the card at the top?

Signed in, your practice card: the next video, picked for you, and one START button.

Which video comes next for my level?

Follow a playlist: videos in order, from the first to the last. Playlists › Explore, then pick your level.

Where are the videos I started?

Signed in, … in progress → at the end of the title line shows them. All of them are in History.

Where are the videos I finished?

Still in the list, with Done, after the ones you haven't finished. Filters › Status › Finished shows only them.

The list ends

Scroll down and press Show more videos: 24 more each time.

Can I keep my filters?

Yes: they are in the page address. Bookmark it.
