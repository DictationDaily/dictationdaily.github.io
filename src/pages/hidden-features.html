Slow down the audio Hidden setting

The YouTube player has its own speed setting, and lessons keep working at any speed: the clip still loops at the slower speed and still stops at the end of the sentence.

  1. 1
    During a lesson, click the ⚙ gear in the top-right corner of the video.
  2. 2
    Choose Speed.
  3. 3
    Pick 0.5x, or use − for finer steps (e.g. 0.75x).
💡
Try a hard clip at 0.75x, then Undo and do it again at normal speed. Going above 1x is good practice for advanced learners.

Filters in the address bar Hidden setting

Every filter can be set from the address, which is handy on phones where the filter panel is hidden, or for bookmarks.

ParameterValues
sortLatest (default), Popular, Slow
levelB1, B2, C1, C2
durationunder-4-minutes, 4-20-minutes, over-20-minutes

Combine them with &: dictationdaily.com/videos?sort=Slow&level=B2&duration=4-20-minutes

ℹ️
Use the capitals shown. Lower case (level=b1) still filters the videos, but the matching button on the left won't light up. An unknown value is simply ignored.

Open a video by its YouTube ID Hidden

A lesson's address uses the same ID as YouTube. If you're watching youtube.com/watch?v=g4IAa8wZlqU, try dictationdaily.com/videos/g4IAa8wZlqU. It works for every video in the library; other videos show a “page not found” message.

Start from the video player Tip

Pressing the YouTube ▶ play button does the same as Start: the lesson begins at the current clip.

Your saved place is the last clip you answered Good to know

The app remembers the last clip you got right, not the furthest. Re-answering clip 5 after reaching clip 30 moves your saved place back to clip 5. Full explanation.

Also: your place loads a moment after the lesson page opens. Wait a second before pressing Start, or the lesson may begin at clip 1.

Clips repeat three times Good to know

If you don't answer, a clip plays up to 3 times in total, then waits silently. Tab or Replay plays it again any number of times. Clips you've already answered don't replay by themselves when you go back to them.

Clips are trimmed for you Behind the scenes

Each clip starts about a quarter of a second before the first word and ends about a third of a second after the last, but never far enough to catch the previous or next speaker. If you seek the video outside the clip, it snaps back.

Subtitles stay off Good to know

YouTube captions are switched off during a lesson, and switched off again if you turn them on, so the answer can't appear on the video. You'll see the full transcript as soon as you answer correctly.

The hint hides while you type Tip

As soon as you type or press Replay, the hint disappears so you can listen without reading. Press Enter to see the next hint. Want to check one word? Type up to that word and press Enter: if it's right, the hint points at the next missing word.

Listen again with highlighted words Tip

After a correct answer, press Tab: the clip replays and each word lights up as it's spoken. It's the best way to hear how words link together in fast speech.

Learning time only counts active practice Good to know

Time stops at your last keystroke on a clip. Thinking before pressing Enter isn't counted, and neither are skipped clips. Details.

Streak days follow UTC Good to know

Streak days change at midnight UTC, not your local midnight. See when that is where you live.

Video quality and data Tip

On a slow or metered connection, choose ⚙ → Quality in the player and pick a low resolution. You only need the sound.

Close works like Back Good to know

Close goes to the previous page in your browser history. If you opened the lesson from a link elsewhere, that's where you'll go. Click the DD logo to go to the library instead.

Watch-only videos Tip

Videos without exercises still play normally, so you can watch them like on YouTube.

What you can't change

There's no settings page. These are fixed for everyone:

  • the number of automatic repeats (3)
  • the answer rules (capitals and punctuation ignored, spelling must match)
  • showing one mistake at a time
  • the UTC day boundary for streaks
