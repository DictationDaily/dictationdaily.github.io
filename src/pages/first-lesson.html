Every video is split into short clips, usually one sentence or part of a sentence. A 2-minute talk has about 45 clips; a 15-minute talk about 200. The lesson plays the whole video in order, so you never miss anything between two clips.

Listen→ Type→ Check→ Fix the hint→ Correct!→ Next clip

The lesson screen

  • Top bar: the logo (back to the library) and the video title.
  • Left: the YouTube player.
  • Right: the dictation area. Before you start it shows a short reminder.
  • Bottom bar (once started): your position and Close. See Moving around a lesson.

Press Start. (Enter works too once you've clicked the instructions box.) You can also just press play on the video.

Step 1: Listen

The clip plays and the video pauses at its end. If you don't answer, it repeats automatically, up to 3 times in total, then waits. Press Replay or Tab to hear it again whenever you like.

ℹ️
YouTube subtitles are switched off during a lesson, so you can't read the answer off the video. If you drag the video outside the current clip, it jumps back to the clip.

Step 2: Type what you hear

Capital letters and punctuation don't matter. Spelling does. The exact rules are on Checking answers and hints.

Step 3: Check

Press Enter or Check. If a word is wrong, a hint appears and the wrong word is selected for you, so you can type straight over it.

Fix it and press Enter again. How to read hints →

Step 4: You are correct!

The full sentence appears. As the clip plays, each word is highlighted as it's spoken, which helps you hear how words run together.

Finishing the lesson

After the last clip, the rest of the video plays as a listen part, then you'll see:

  • Back to browse returns to the library.
  • Redo the lesson starts again from clip 1.

Videos without exercises

A few videos don't have a transcript yet. They open and play, but instead of the dictation area you'll see “Dictation exercises for this video are not available yet”. Pick another video.
