In exercises, the shortcuts work while the cursor is in the text box. It's selected automatically on a computer; if Tab moves focus instead of replaying, click inside the box. In listen parts, they work anywhere on the page.

A fast routine

  1. 1
    Listen. Press Tab as often as you need.
  2. 2
    Type, then Enter.
  3. 3
    The wrong word is already selected: type over it, Enter.
  4. 4
    Tab once more to hear it with the words highlighted, then Enter for the next clip.
