A fast routine

1️⃣

Listen

Press Tab as often as you need.

2️⃣

Type, then Enter

The wrong word is selected for you.

3️⃣

Fix it, Enter

Repeat until it's right.

4️⃣

Then Space

Go to the next sentence.

⏎
Before the lesson starts, Enter presses Start.
📖
While a word card is open, these keys wait; Esc closes the card instead of skipping.
↩️
After a ✓, Esc is UNDO: your answer is cleared and the sentence plays again. While the microphone listens (a voice answer or saying the sentence), Esc drops the recording. The microphone itself has no key: tap it.
🎤
In Shadowing: Space starts or stops the microphone and continues, Tab replays the line, Esc undoes, Enter plays your take. On the finish screen, Enter is Practise again.
☝️
Keys not working? Click inside the text box first.
